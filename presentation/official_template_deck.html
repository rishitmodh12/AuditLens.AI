01 PROBLEM

Problem

What problem are you solving and for whom? Keep it to 2-3 lines.

The Problem

Enterprise customer SLAs promise 99.9% uptime (max 1.0 hr downtime/month) with ₹75,000/hr penalties, while upstream cloud vendor MSAs allow 4.0-hour maintenance windows with zero penalty. Furthermore, under CERT-In Directions (2022), statutory breach reports are required within 6 hours, while internal CISO notification takes 30 minutes. Uncoordinated contracts create severe financial leakage and statutory regulatory penalties under IT Act §70B.

Who Is Affected

Enterprise Legal Counsel, FinOps Teams, SRE Incident Commanders, and CISO Compliance Officers in Indian IT, fintech, and SaaS companies. Without automated cross-document intelligence, organizations spend hundreds of manual legal hours per incident, fail to recover vendor liabilities, and risk criminal compliance citations.

02 SOLUTION

Solution

Explain your idea simply, and what makes it different.

What We Built

AuditLens AI: An automated cross-contract due diligence and discrepancy engine. It concurrently indexes customer SLAs, vendor agreements, and incident response policies into MongoDB Atlas Vector Search, automatically reconciling service credit penalties (e.g. ₹75,000 for a 2-hour outage under Section 4.2), flag-checking notification clocks, and scanning for cross-contract contradictions.

What Makes It Unique

• Native 70/30 Hybrid Search: Fuses Atlas dense vector search with exact lexical token matching for clause numbers (§4.2) and ₹ amounts.
• Code-Level Verbatim Quote Verification: Every citation is substring-checked against Atlas raw chunks (🛡️ Verbatim Verified badge).
• Deterministic Compliance Health Scoring: Formula-derived: 100 - (20×High) - (10×Med) - (10×Unverified).
• Strict Hallucination Defense & Safe Refusal: Clean refusal on out-of-scope queries (e.g. drone surveillance).
